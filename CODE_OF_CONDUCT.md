# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, review carefully, and keep this
organization's public-facing profile and branding accurate, useful, and representative of Salesgee.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Treat public-facing content with care. This repository is often a visitor's first impression of
  Salesgee on GitHub.
- Ask for clarification when a change or decision is unclear.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Adding secrets, credentials, customer data, merchant data, or internal-only details without
  approval.
- Dismissing accuracy, accessibility, or brand-consistency concerns without discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, reject, or request changes to contributions that violate this standard.
Security-sensitive concerns should be escalated through the approved internal team channel.
