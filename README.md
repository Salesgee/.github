# Salesgee

Salesgee is an all-in-one ecommerce management platform for African sellers and product-based
businesses.

It brings the daily work of running an online store into one dashboard: orders, staff, customers,
inventory, finance, storefronts, sales pages, marketing campaigns, analytics, and integrations.

## What Salesgee Helps Businesses Do

- Launch an online storefront without writing code.
- Capture and manage orders from one operating dashboard.
- Assign orders to sales reps automatically and track team performance.
- Manage customers, products, inventory, reports, and store finances.
- Connect existing ecommerce workflows, including WooCommerce.
- Grow distribution through Salesgee's built-in Product Hunt marketplace.
