# Salesgee

Salesgee is the ecommerce operating system built for African entrepreneurs, online sellers, and
product-based businesses.

We help sellers move beyond spreadsheets, scattered chats, and manual follow-ups by bringing orders,
staff, customers, inventory, storefronts, payments, analytics, and growth tools into one coordinated
workspace.

## Built For How Serious Sellers Work

- **Store owners and product brands** running ecommerce operations across social channels,
  storefronts, and existing websites.
- **Sales teams and operations managers** who need clear assignment, tracking, and performance
  visibility for every order.
- **Growing businesses** that want customer records, inventory, finance, campaigns, and reports in
  one place instead of spread across disconnected tools.

## What You Can Run With Salesgee

- Online storefronts and sales pages.
- Order management with automatic assignment.
- Staff roles, permissions, and performance tracking.
- Customer management and order history.
- Inventory tracking and product management.
- Analytics, reports, finance overview, and store wallet.
- WooCommerce order capture and connected ecommerce workflows.
- Product Hunt, Salesgee's internal marketplace for supplier and reseller growth.
